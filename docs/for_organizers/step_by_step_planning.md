# Step-by-Step Guide

<img class="full-width-img" src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/photos/help_desk_presentation2.jpg" alt="People at a Data Help Desk presentation" width="500">

1. Get in contact with local group or conference organizers
1. Confirm space and general timing (when the conference is, when and where the help desk will be set up)
    - Demo space? Engagement space?
    - Space signage
1. Create an advertisting plan and start advertising
    - Start with basic ad
    - Update/Create website page
- Couple months out
1. Create volunteer form
1. Call for volunteers/demoers around abstract submission deadline
    - Update site
    - Advertise this call
    - Leave open for about a month
1. Create volunteer schedule
- One month out
1. Advertise specific demos/times
1. Gather printed materials
1. Create slide deck
1. Gather swag
1. Confirm volunteer schedule when conference schedule comes out
- Conference time
1. Create WhatsApp
1. Gather photos
1. Gather questions
- Post conference
1. Question follow-up

- Question Forms - digital or physical?
